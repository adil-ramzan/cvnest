## The short version

- You don't need an account to use CVNest, and we don't offer accounts today.
- The resumes you create are stored **in your own browser on your device**. CVNest doesn't send your resume content or photos to our servers.
- PDFs are created by your own browser. Your resume isn't uploaded to make them.
- We don't use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.
- Like any website, our hosting provider processes basic technical information (such as your IP address) to deliver pages to you.

## Information stored in your browser

When you use the resume editor, CVNest saves the following **only in your browser**, using your browser's local storage and IndexedDB:

- The resume content you enter: for example your name, job title, contact details, work history, education, skills and any other sections.
- An optional profile photo, which your browser resizes before storing.
- Editor preferences, such as which resume was last open, zoom level, the last open section, and when you last downloaded a backup.

This information stays on your device. We can't see it, access it, recover it or delete it for you. You control it:

- **To delete it**, clear this site's data in your browser settings, or delete individual resumes in the editor.
- **To keep a copy**, use "Download backup" in the editor. The backup file is created by your browser and saved to your device; it isn't sent to us.

Things to be aware of:

- Browser storage isn't encrypted. Anyone with access to your browser profile could read it. Avoid using CVNest on shared or public computers.
- Clearing browsing data, using a private window, or some browsers' automatic storage clean-up can permanently remove your saved resumes. Download a backup regularly.
- Browser extensions you install, browser synchronization features, and any cloud service you save files to (such as a downloaded PDF or backup) are controlled by you and those providers, not by CVNest.
